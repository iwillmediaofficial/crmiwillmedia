Standard Operating Procedure

1. System Overview: The Two Core Roles

The CRM separates everyday execution from managerial oversight so everyone stays focused and efficient.

👤 Staff Role (The User / Doer) Team Member

Primary Mission: Complete daily deliverables, clock accurate session timers, handle blockers, and contact assigned leads.

  • Work Deliverables: Receives work assignments with an instant audio chime alert. Starts and stops personal timer.
  • Holds / Blockers: Can mark tasks "Pending" if waiting for client assets, logos, or approvals.
  • Leads Follow-Up: Sees assigned leads and clicks WhatsApp to chat directly with prospective clients.
  • Protected View: Financial records, billing invoices, and rates are hidden to ensure privacy.

🛡️ Admin Role (The Manager / Overseer) Management

Primary Mission: Manage clients, assign work items to team members, monitor live timers in real-time, and manage billing retainers.

  • Master Control: Create clients, assign deliverables to team members, update priorities, and set deadlines.
  • Live Oversight: Sees a real-time banner of who is working right now with ticking stopwatches.
  • Billing Management: Creates monthly retainers and manages invoices across INR (₹), AED (د.إ), and USD ($).
  • Safety & Authority: Exclusively deletes work items, creates staff accounts, and views financial reports.

Staff only need to know 3 buttons: Start Timer when starting work, Mark Pending if blocked by client feedback, and Complete when the deliverable is finished. The CRM handles notifications, timesheets, and invoices automatically.

Daily Operations

3. Daily Work Deliverables Cycle

Designed for clear accountability: staff always know what to work on, when it's due, and time spent.

Step A: Admin Assigns Work

Admin opens /work and clicks "Assign Work":

  • Selects Client & Deliverable Title (e.g. "Brand Video Reel").
  • Assigns to designated Staff Member.
  • Sets Due Date and Priority (Low, Normal, High, Urgent).
  • Optional: Enters Billable Amount (masked from staff).

Step B: Staff Hears Audio Alert

Staff receives immediate real-time alert:

  • A pleasant audio chime plays through computer speakers.
  • A notification toast displays the new assignment.
  • Task appears inside the staff member's workload card under "Active".
⏱️ The 4 Work Item Statuses

1. Assigned

Waiting in queue to be started.

Action: "Start Timer"

2. In Progress

Staff is actively working. Live ticker runs.

Action: Work on project

3. Pending (Hold)

Paused due to blocker or client delay.

Action: "Resume Work"

4. Completed

Milestone finished and delivered.

Action: Auto-generates bill

How Timers and Blockers Work

🟢 Starting the Timer

• Click "Start Timer" on any task.
• Top navigation bar turns purple with a pulsing green dot tracking 01:24:35.
• Starting a new timer automatically stops any previous active timer (prevents double billing).

⚠️ Blocker / Hold Reason

• If waiting for client approval, click "Mark Pending".
• Type the hold reason (e.g. "Waiting for client logo asset") and expected resume date.
• Keeps everyone aligned so deadlines can be adjusted.

✅ Completing the Task

• When deliverable is done, click "Complete".
• Total accumulated time is permanently recorded.
• If the deliverable is billable, an invoice is automatically placed into /billing for admin approval!

📋 Workload Section Filters (All • Active • On going • Pending • Done)
Staff cards load 5 deliverables at a time with a "Load More" button for clean page loading. Staff can filter deliverables instantly using the top filter buttons.

4. Billing, Retainers & Calendar Overview

Cashflow tracking supporting INR (₹), AED (د.إ), and USD ($).

🔄 Monthly Retainer Contracts

Set up once, auto-generates forever:
• In /billing under Retainers, create a template (e.g. "Monthly Digital Marketing — ₹30,000 due 5th of every month").
• The CRM automatically generates the individual monthly occurrence bills on schedule without disturbing the master contract!

📅 Visual Monthly Billing Calendar

Color-coded day-by-day cashflow:
• Open /calendar to see all bills mapped onto calendar days.
• 🟢 Emerald = Paid Invoices
• 🟡 Amber = Pending Invoices
• 🔴 Rose = Overdue Invoices
